Tips

How to do the things that aren't obvious, by task. Each tip says where to go.

Something every Friday except the first

In Add event, choose Repeats: Monthly, by weekday (1st Tue…), pick Friday, and tick 2nd, 3rd, 4th and 5th (leave 1st and Last unticked). For the thing that happens on the 1st Friday instead, make a second event with only 1st ticked.

Skip or move one date of a repeating event

On the Events list, open that date's entry. Change this date moves it or changes its time, place or notes; Skip this date takes that one date off. The rest of the series stays as it was. To undo, open the series with Edit the whole series and use Put back as normal beside the date.

On the shelf, Move and Delete on a repeating event also affect that one date only.

Change a repeating event from a date onward

Open the first date that should change and choose Change from this date on (or Delete from this date on). Earlier dates keep their old details, so the history stays right. Edit the whole series changes every date, past ones included.

Be reminded days ahead, such as for birthdays

In Categories, open the category (for example Birthday) and set Reminder for all-day events, such as birthdays, for example 2 days. The shelf then shows "Kate's birthday is Thursday", then "Kate's birthday is tomorrow", then "Kate's birthday today". One setting covers every event in that category. A single event can still use its own reminder, or none, in its event form.

Mark an event out of town

In the event form, tick Away from home under Out of town. The shelf then shows an "Out of town" tag with a car on it wherever the event appears. For a repeating event it applies to every date. If the event uses its category's reminder, it is reminded earlier: Out of town reminder, in days before in Settings (5 days to begin with). At a Google import, a place the calendar hasn't seen asks Local or out of town? once, and the answer is remembered for that place; towns on the Local towns list in Settings never ask, and an event with no location counts as local. You can change or forget an answer under Places answered at import.

Household reminders: "I did it"

In Reminders, each reminder has two choices for when it isn't done:

  • Take it off the display at the end of the day: for things that only matter that day, like the garbage cans.
  • Keep showing it until someone taps "I did it": for things that must not be forgotten, like the A/C filters. It never shows twice, even if the next one comes round first.

Choose Doesn't repeat for a one-time reminder, such as calling the plumber; once done, it's finished for good. On the shelf, "I did it" gives a few seconds to Undo.

One-tap titles on the shelf's Add form

In Quick titles, keep the titles the household adds most (up to about 10). Each can also pick a category. Suggest from my events lists titles you use often.

Get back something deleted

Anything deleted, here or on the shelf, stays in Recently deleted for 3 days (the last 20 deletions). Use Restore to put it back.

Google import

  • Import → Fetch from Google only reads Google; nothing is ever changed there. Nothing reaches the calendar until you add it in the review.
  • Title rules on the import source page set the category and people for new events whose title contains a word, for example "birthday".
  • Not in Google lists events added here that aren't in Google yet. Add to Google opens Google with the details filled in; you press Save there. Not needed in Google takes an event off the list for good.

Keep a copy of the calendar

The server copies the calendar every night. For an extra copy of your own, use Settings → Download a backup. Keep the file private: it holds the household's events.

If the shelf looks wrong

  • Amber bar "Not updated since…": the shelf can't reach the calendar. It keeps showing the last schedule it received; adding, moving and deleting wait until it's back.
  • Health button amber or red: tap it to see which reading needs attention. The full detail is on the Health page.
  • Black screen: it's probably dark hours; a touch wakes it. Dark hours are in Settings.
